# Mesh asset library

Cooked by `cooker/cook.py` into `assets/library/<id>/` (meters, Z-up, footprint centered at the
origin, lowest point at z=0). Every asset is CC0 1.0 (public domain); credit is given as a courtesy.

Each folder holds `meta.json`, `visual.glb` (Z-up, the page rotates it), `visual_<k>.obj` (MuJoCo
visual geoms), `hull_<k>.obj` (CoACD convex pieces, MuJoCo collision) and `thumb.png`.

Kenney kits: Furniture Kit 2.0, Survival Kit, City Kit (Roads), each shipping a License.txt that
states Creative Commons Zero. Poly Haven: all assets are CC0 (https://polyhaven.com/license). Kenney
models were rescaled with `--target-height` to real-world sizes; Poly Haven models are already in
meters (steel_shelves was exported at 10x and was scaled to its listed 2.14 m height).

Later additions: Kenney Food Kit, Mini Market, Mini Arcade and Nature Kit (same CC0 License.txt);
Quaternius Ultimate House Interior, Ultimate Nature, Survival, Ultimate RPG and Cyberpunk Game Kit
packs, each listed as "License CC0" on its quaternius.com pack page (files taken from the
github.com/trebeljahr/quaternius-showcase mirror); a few Poly Pizza models whose pages state "Public
Domain (CC0)". Poly Haven models used for the 1k glTF were first decimated in Blender to about 4000
triangles (UVs kept) and cooked with `--texture-size 256 --max-faces 3000` to keep each folder under
1 MB; metal_trash_can, vintage_suitcase and fire_hydrant ship as sets, so only one item was kept.
book_pile is five Quaternius Book1_Closed copies stacked, and shoe_pile is two Boots and two
Slippers (Isa Lousberg) arranged together, before cooking. The Kenney, Quaternius and Poly Pizza
kits store sRGB colors as glTF's linear base color, so they are cooked with `--srgb-factors`.

The procedural assets (an office's furniture, gear and robots) are built from boxes, cylinders and
short revolves by [tools/procedural_assets.py](tools/procedural_assets.py) (no outside model), then
cooked with `--up z --srgb-factors`: `python tools/procedural_assets.py <dir>`, then `cook.py` each
`<dir>/<id>.glb`. They are this project's own work, CC0 1.0 like the rest.
